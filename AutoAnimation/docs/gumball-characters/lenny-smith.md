# Lenny Smith

## Appearance
He is a short green man, with very thick eyebrows, and a body made up of cubes. His arms attach to axes and end in dents functioning as hands. The character resembles a Lego figure and consistently wears work clothes with the same expression, except when laughing or scared. From season 4 onward, a redesign removed the shadow separating the head protrusion, making it less defined.

## Personality
Lenny demonstrates friendliness toward coworkers but maintains distance from others, such as laughing at Richard's struggles. While appearing competent professionally, he harbors a secret desire to run naked through forests. The only reason he does not do this is because of his paycheck.
