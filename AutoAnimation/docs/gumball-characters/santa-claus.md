# Santa Claus

## Appearance
Before regaining his memories, after being hit by a car, Santa was filthy and smelly, and wore tattered clothing that has vague similarities to his actual attire. He had wild, untrimmed white hair covering his entire face and a curly beard. After being hit by a second car which restored his memories, his appearance completely changes -- he matches the depictions of Santa in legends completely, with neater facial hair, visible eyes and nose, and clean, neat, and shiny clothing.

## Personality
Before regaining his memories, Santa believes he is simply a homeless man and has no understanding of who he actually is. He found the children's attempts to restore his memory annoying and experienced severe depression after learning Santa was supposedly nonexistent. The real Santa is a jolly man that cares deeply about the safety of other people, even willing to sacrifice his own expensive, high-tech sled to save others. He is deeply committed to his mission, working hard to make sure all the good boys and girls receive gifts on Christmas.
