# Bennett

## Appearance
Bennett is a humanoid cumulus cloud the same as Yuki and Masami. He dresses in a matte dark grey tailcoat with black bowtie, and sporting a majestic curly long hair. Notably, Bennett does not wear shoes; rather his feet are a levitating cloud similar to Mr. Yoshida. He possesses cloud physiology (dispersing when touched and quickly reforming) and materialization abilities (creating objects from clouds through his breath).
