# Gumball Watterson

## Appearance
Gumball is a light blue cat with six whiskers (five typically visible). He wears grey trousers, coupled with a tan sweater, which has brown cuffs and a brown collar. He lacks shoes and is the only Watterson without visible eyelashes normally. Season 2 brought a larger head, whiskers, and rounder eyes. Season 3 standardized permanently round eyes.

Originally intended as a blue dog placeholder, creators reconsidered and chose black cat to symbolize bad luck, but this proved too restrictive narratively. The final decision was blue, which creator Ben Bocquelet appreciated for its "70's-80's Japanese mascot" aesthetic and rarity on feline characters. Gumball's age evolved from 10 (2009) to 11 (2010) to the finalized 12 years old.

Since 2013, promotional materials outside the US feature Gumball in a red/white varsity jacket, white shirt with blue collar, blue pants, and red sneakers -- never shown in-series. Creator Bocquelet stated marketing found this "cooler." Italian brand Octopus Brand created replica jackets in 2020.

## Personality
Gumball demonstrates imagination despite mediocre academics, formulating mischievous schemes and displaying hidden intelligence through vocabulary, cooking, and musical abilities. He maintains optimism despite cynicism toward society, supporting others through difficult circumstances. He is fiercely protective of family and siblings, becoming over-protective when loved ones face threats. His sarcastic nature produces both harmless and blunt remarks impulsively. A significant ego makes him sensitive to criticism and drives competitive jealousy, though he recognizes errors. Overall, he balances cleverness with emotional reactivity.
