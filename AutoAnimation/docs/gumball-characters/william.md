# William

## Appearance
William is a flying eyeball with white, bird-like wings which are his only means of locomotion. His body consists of a single green eye. When enraged or using powers, his eye will sometimes turn red and the pupil becomes a slit. His pupil can appear dilated or non-dilated, and most kids find him creepy.

## Personality
William functions as Miss Simian's personal snitch who spies on students. He rarely speaks except when whispering to Miss Simian. Despite his unsettling appearance and inability to communicate normally, he is actually a decent guy most of the time, showing honor and friendliness. However, when crossed, he displays a cold, calculating side, as shown when he manipulated Gumball and Darwin into fighting. "The Agent" episode reveals his cruel and spiteful side, where he steals possessions without remorse and holds strong grudges, willing to exact revenge for minor slights.
