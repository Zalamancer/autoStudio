# Hector Jotunheim

## Appearance
Hector is an enormous, gorilla-like giant with bluish-purple skin covered in shaggy, orange and pink fur, with only his hands, feet, and buttocks being bare. His face resembles an ape's and was finally revealed in "The Butterfly." His size varies dramatically between episodes, sometimes showing only his legs and feet, other times reaching nearly a thousand feet tall and towering over Elmore. Because of this inconsistency, he does not walk to school; rather, his mom, Mrs. Jotunheim, rides her broom to his school and then teleports him there. In the early prototype design, his legs featured more varied colors and lighter blue skin.

## Personality
Despite his menacing appearance, Hector is actually very soft and kind, quite literally being a gentle giant. He avoids hurting others, though he has accidentally stepped on people. Later episodes reveal that other kids find him boring. When attempting to prove otherwise, his "exciting" activities prove lame -- reading actionless comics, taking naps, playing with his dead hamster. His mother forbids extreme emotional expression because when you excite a giant, destruction ensues. This restraint prevents him from doing exciting things. When encouraged by Gumball to defy his mother, Hector rampages through town, ultimately destroying 75% of Elmore, costing over $800,000 in damages. In "The Potion," he drinks a shrinking potion but becomes depressed and insulting as a small being, calling others "cockroaches."
