# Jealousy

## Appearance
Not available (no dedicated Appearance section). The introductory text describes Jealousy as a pure manifestation of jealousy that can possess anyone or anything, causing the victim to turn a shade of green and gain its face as well as its voice. His green color is a nod to the expression "green with envy."
