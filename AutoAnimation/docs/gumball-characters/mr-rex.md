# Mr. Rex

## Appearance
Mr. Rex is a Tyrannosaurus rex who towers above most inhabitants of Elmore, including his daughter Tina. He possesses thick, brownish-green scales all over his body and huge, black claws on his feet. His facial features include an enormous mouth filled with rows of razor-sharp teeth, and piercing, red and yellow eyes. His eyes are positioned closer together and more forward-facing than Tina's. A scar over his right eye, revealed in "The Routine," indicates that eye is blinded -- possibly from a confrontation with Nicole.

## Personality
Mr. Rex demonstrates extreme protectiveness toward his daughter and home. Unlike Tina, he is very animalistic and brutal, preferring to settle disputes by fighting instead of talking. However, Tina mentions in "The Blame" that he plays video games with her, suggesting a gentler side exists beneath his fierce exterior. He generally maintains a positive relationship with his daughter despite his aggressive demeanor toward outsiders.
