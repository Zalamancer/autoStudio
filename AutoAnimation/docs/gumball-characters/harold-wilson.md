# Harold Wilson

## Appearance
Harold shares a humanoid form resembling his son, featuring the same blue, red, and orange gradient color scheme. He sports a blue mustache, and wears a white collar with a pink tie. Unlike his family members, he notably goes without shoes, headbands, or wristbands.

## Personality
Harold demonstrates contrasting behavioral patterns. The episode "The Cycle" reveals he enjoys tormenting Richard, a dynamic dating back to their high school years. Like his son, younger Harold was portrayed as a womanizer who attempted to court Nicole. Despite being financially comfortable, he abandoned his wife for an attractive younger woman named Jazelle when believing himself to have become a billionaire, suggesting he prioritizes material wealth and romantic pursuits. However, Harold claims restrictions prevent him from being his authentic self at home. His professional background involves medical work; he successfully posed as a psychologist to Richard and is ultimately confirmed to be a psychotherapist, as established when Gumball and Darwin seek his professional assistance.
