# Carlton

## Appearance
Carlton is a blond human boy teenager. He wears a pale blue sweater with a pink sweater tied around his neck, and white shorts with a green sweater tied around his waist.

## Personality
Carlton thinks he is very hardcore, more hardcore than Gumball and Darwin (and possibly than all of Elmore). He is very boastful, and can be a real bully along with Troy. He exhibits ignorance and limited intelligence, though he demonstrates more logic than his companion Troy in later episodes.
