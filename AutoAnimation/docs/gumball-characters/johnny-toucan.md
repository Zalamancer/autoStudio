# Johnny Toucan

## Appearance
Johnny Toucan is a blue and yellow toucan with yellow hair on top. He has an orange, blue, and dark pink beak on his face. He wears brown shoes on his small feet. He wears a bright pink polo shirt with beige short jeans, and a waist pouch. He has some accessories with sunglasses on top of his head, a rope necklace around his neck, and a watch on his wrist. He has a long, thickly muscular body, which makes him taller than most other characters.
