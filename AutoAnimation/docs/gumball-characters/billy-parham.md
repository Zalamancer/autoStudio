# Billy Parham

## Appearance
Billy is a blue blob shaped like an egg. Like other Elmore residents, he has standard features including two eyes, a mouth, two legs, and two arms. His lips have a pink-peach coloration.

## Personality
Billy shares his mother's condescending attitude and superiority complex. He frequently ridicules others, sometimes escalating to bullying behavior. The character demonstrates intelligence and cultural refinement alongside social awkwardness. He communicates using sophisticated vocabulary appropriate for his age. He habitually questions his mother by beginning with "Mother..." about surrounding events, though her responses tend toward dismissiveness or insults. Despite this dynamic, Billy remains deeply attached to his mother. He can temporarily set aside his snobbish demeanor, particularly when enjoying compatible company. However, he typically reverts to condescending behavior when certain triggers arise. Like typical children, he exhibits emotional responses to fear or distress, including crying when intimidated.
