# Margaret Robinson

## Appearance
Mrs. Robinson shares her husband's basic body structure but with notably larger hips and a slimmer frame. Her head connects via a neck rather than being directly attached to her body.

She sports a voluminous blonde yarn-like wig styled in a large beehive with bangs, adorned with a light-brown and white polka-dotted bow. Her features include two beady eyes framed by purple eye shadow and a large, protruding nose similar to her husband's.

Her typical wardrobe consists of a white and brown zig-zagging shirt, dark brown jeans, and white pumps. In "The Wonderfully Weird World of Gumball," she receives fabric textures.

## Personality
Mrs. Robinson rarely displays happiness and communicates primarily through grunts and resentful muttering rather than clear speech. Despite loving her husband deeply, they constantly bicker and quarrel. She demonstrated disloyalty by nearly marrying another man when Mr. Robinson fell ill and enjoys witnessing his failures.

Her husband describes her nature as fundamentally mysterious: "Margaret is, and always will be, a mystery. Nothing happened in her life to justify the way she is." Her manufacturer's tag reveals she is "Seventy percent cotton, thirty percent polyester and made in the fiery pits of the underworld," suggesting her malevolent disposition stems from her very creation rather than trauma.

Margaret demonstrates significant infidelity throughout the series, abandoning her husband for other men on multiple occasions.
