# Sluzzle Dude

## Appearance
Unlike the joyful, more stylistic appearance of Santa Claus corresponding to Christmas, Sluzzle Dude is conceived to take on a more primitive appearance. This holiday figure is depicted to be a grey humanoid; he has green eyes, a beard, and a beanie hat; his attire also includes a black sweater, denim pants, and black shoes.
