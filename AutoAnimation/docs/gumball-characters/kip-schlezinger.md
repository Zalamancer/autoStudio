# Kip Schlezinger

He is a live action male human dressed in a black suit over a white shirt. He also bolsters a blue tie, though on occasion he has worn a red tie (additionally, in the rough cut of "The News" and in "The Web" he wears a striped tie). In "The News," he is a giant live-action puppet made out of newspapers, instead of a normal human.
