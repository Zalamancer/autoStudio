# Idaho

## Appearance
Idaho is an anthropomorphic potato with a face, arms, and legs drawn in black marker. He ranks among the show's smallest characters, with only viruses, bacteria, microbes, and insects being smaller. His parents, uncle, and a few other characters match his size. In season 2, his face becomes smaller and his coloring darkens slightly.

## Personality
Idaho, raised in the countryside, follows beliefs and mannerisms similar to the Amish, and speaks with a Southern twang. While not particularly intelligent, he demonstrates friendliness and optimism. His character embodies rural values and a positive outlook despite his modest intellect.
