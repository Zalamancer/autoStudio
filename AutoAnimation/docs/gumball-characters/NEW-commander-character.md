# Commander Character — TAWOG Original

A political leader / commander character in The Amazing World of Gumball's art style. Humanoid, cool outfit, likeable and authoritative.

---

## Marshal — The Commander

### Species / Design Basis
A tall, lean humanoid cat — same species family as Nicole and Gumball Watterson, but a steel-blue/gunmetal color that sets him apart. Gumball-style flat 2D with thick outlines, simple shapes, and exaggerated proportions (big head, lanky body, large expressive eyes).

### Appearance
Marshal is a steel-blue cat with sharp, angular features — pointed ears that stand perfectly upright, narrow confident eyes with bright amber irises, and a small sharp-toothed grin that always looks like he knows something you don't. He's tall and lean with long legs, broad shoulders, and a straight-backed posture that makes him look even taller.

**The outfit is the centerpiece:**
- **Long military-style coat** — deep charcoal black with a standing collar, falling to just below his knees. Gold trim along the edges and cuffs. The coat is always open, flowing behind him when he walks, giving him a dramatic silhouette. Two rows of gold buttons down the front (he never buttons it — too cool for that).
- **Underneath:** A simple dark turtleneck, fitted and clean.
- **Belt:** A thick leather utility belt worn at an angle across his hips, with a single gold buckle shaped like a star. No weapons or tools on it — it's purely aesthetic, but he acts like it holds "classified equipment."
- **Boots:** Tall black military boots that go up to mid-calf, with a slight heel that adds to his height. Always polished.
- **Gloves:** Fingerless black gloves — practical but stylish.
- **One signature accessory:** A single gold epaulette on his left shoulder with short tassels that sway when he moves. Just one — he says the other one is "for when he earns it," implying he holds himself to impossible standards.

His tail is long and expressive — it betrays his emotions even when his face stays cool. When he's nervous, it puffs up. When he's genuinely happy (rare in public), it curls into a question mark shape.

### Personality
Marshal transferred to Elmore from "somewhere he doesn't talk about" and immediately established himself as the most composed person in a town full of chaos. He doesn't yell, doesn't panic, doesn't run. When everyone is screaming, Marshal is standing with his arms crossed, coat billowing, assessing the situation with half-lidded eyes. He's the guy who walks TOWARD the explosion.

He's a natural leader — not because he demands authority, but because he's unnervingly calm when everything falls apart. People follow him because he seems like he has a plan, even when he absolutely doesn't. His secret: **he's improvising 90% of the time.** His "strategies" are just confident-sounding sentences he makes up on the spot. The twist is they usually work anyway, because his calm demeanor keeps everyone else from panicking.

Despite the cool exterior, Marshal is deeply awkward one-on-one. He's great at commanding a crowd but terrible at normal conversation. He doesn't know how to be casual — he'll say things like "I appreciate your tactical friendship" instead of "thanks for hanging out." He eats lunch alone not because he's mysterious, but because he doesn't know how to ask someone to sit with him without making it sound like a military briefing.

He has a soft spot for anyone being bullied or left out — this is the one thing that breaks his composure. He'll drop the commander act instantly and get genuinely angry (tail puffing up, ears flattening, voice cracking) if he sees someone being picked on. This is the real Marshal underneath the coat.

**Likeable because:** He TRIES so hard to be cool and usually pulls it off, but his moments of awkwardness and genuine kindness make him endearing. He's not a parody of a leader — he's a kid who found that acting like a commander is the only way he knows how to connect with people.

**Running gag:** His coat blows dramatically in the wind at perfect moments — even indoors where there IS no wind. Nobody questions it. Also, he rehearses his "cool lines" in the bathroom mirror and occasionally someone overhears him practicing.

**Contradiction:** Looks like the most confident person in Elmore. Is actually socially anxious and has no idea how to make friends without a chain of command.

### Voice & Speech Patterns
- Low, measured voice — never raises it (when he does, it's a big character moment)
- Speaks in short, decisive sentences: "We move. Now." / "That's handled." / "I've seen worse."
- Accidentally slips into awkward sincerity: "Your presence is... not unpleasant to me."
- When flustered, his sentences get longer and more military-jargon heavy as a defense mechanism
- Signature phrase: "I didn't come here to watch."

### Relationships
- **Gumball:** Fascinated by how Gumball survives pure chaos through dumb luck. Studies him like a field commander studying an unpredictable enemy. Secretly thinks Gumball might be a genius. Gumball thinks Marshal is "the coolest guy ever" and keeps trying to get him to do normal kid stuff (play video games, eat junk food) — which Marshal is terrible at but secretly enjoys.
- **Darwin:** The only person Marshal can relax around, because Darwin doesn't judge. Darwin treats him normally, which is both refreshing and terrifying.
- **Nicole:** Recognizes her as a fellow "commander type" and treats her with genuine respect — the only adult he does this for. Nicole sees through his act immediately but respects that he uses his composure to help people, not hurt them.
- **Penny:** She tried to befriend him normally and he panicked and assigned her a "diplomatic envoy" role. She found it funny and plays along, which actually made them friends.
- **Tobias:** Tobias tries to copy Marshal's style and fails spectacularly. Marshal finds this mildly annoying but secretly flattering.

### Key Episode Concepts
1. **"The Transfer"** — Marshal arrives at Elmore Junior High. His dramatic entrance (coat, boots, single epaulette) makes everyone think he's someone important. He leans into it. The episode reveals he's just a normal kid who developed the commander persona to cope with being the new kid at seven different schools.
2. **"The Order"** — Marshal accidentally gives a "command" that the entire school follows, creating escalating chaos. He has to figure out how to undo it without admitting he never had a plan.
3. **"The Coat"** — Someone steals/damages his coat and we see Marshal without it for the first time. He's a completely different person — fidgety, quiet, hunched. The episode is about the line between "fake it till you make it" and hiding who you really are.
4. **"The Friend"** — Marshal tries to ask Gumball to hang out but can only frame it as a "joint reconnaissance mission." Gumball sees right through it and just says "dude, you can just say you wanna hang out." Marshal.exe stops responding.

### Design Notes for NB2 Pipeline
- **Color palette:** Steel-blue/gunmetal fur, amber eyes, charcoal black coat with gold trim, dark turtleneck, black boots and gloves, single gold epaulette with tassels
- **Body shape:** Tall and lean, Gumball-cat proportions but stretched taller. Big head, large eyes, small nose, sharp ears
- **Art style:** Flat 2D, thick outlines, no gradients — standard TAWOG style. The coat's gold trim and epaulette add visual richness without breaking the flat style
- **Animation quirks:** Coat always billowing slightly; tail betrays emotions; ears rotate like radar dishes when alert; single epaulette tassels sway with movement
- **Scale:** Taller than most students, shorter than most adults. Eye-level with Nicole.
- **Expression range:** Default is half-lidded confidence. Key emotions: composed (default), genuinely angry (ears flat, tail puffed), awkward (eyes dart, tail wraps around leg), rare genuine smile (small, lopsided, ears relax)
