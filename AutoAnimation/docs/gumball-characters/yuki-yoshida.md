# Yuki Yoshida

## Appearance
Yuki is a tall, slim humanoid white cloud woman with long "hair." She is usually seen wearing a black business suit, along with black high heels, yellow earrings, purple eyeshadow, and red lipstick. During her encounter with Nicole, she wore a red bodysuit and had braided hair with physical eyes. As a child, she had curly hair with a bow, wore a white gi with black belt and pants, and had visible eyes and pupils unlike her daughter.

## Personality
In childhood, Yuki was friendly and close with Nicole as they studied martial arts together. After losing to Nicole in a final competition, she became offended and left for Japan to train intensively. Seeking revenge, she returned to challenge Nicole through various disruptive actions including flirting with Richard and sabotaging her workplace. During the fight, Yuki's energy attacks caused the roof to collapse on their children, trapping them underneath. This prompted both women to reconcile and save their children. Though they apologized and restored their friendship, the episode "The Gourmet" indicates they still view each other as rivals, with mutual criticism about parenting and lifestyle choices.
