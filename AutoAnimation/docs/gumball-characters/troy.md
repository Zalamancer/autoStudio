# Troy

## Appearance
Troy is a tanned human teenager who wears distinctive layered clothing including a purple sweater with an orange sweater around his neck, white shorts with a cyan sweater at the waist, and cyan sunglasses. His brunette hair features one bleached lock.

## Personality
Troy shares similarities with Carlton, believing himself more hardcore than Gumball and Darwin. However, he is quieter than Carlton while still being bullying. Like Carlton and Coach Kreese, Troy demonstrates ignorance -- continuing to fight even after learning his opponents are not hardcore, then befriending them without them earning his respect through any meaningful actions.
