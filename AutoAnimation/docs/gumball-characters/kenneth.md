# Kenneth

## Appearance
Kenneth was created by mixing together various disgusting things (such as snotty tissues, bird feces, and even regurgitated spoiled milk) into a jar, which was then put into the microwave. The resulting creature is greyish-brown in color with a vaguely humanoid form and a loose, runny body consistency. He has a large mouth with razor-sharp teeth and glowing white eyes when grown to larger size.

## Personality
Kenneth is a malicious and dangerous beast. The only thing he seems to enjoy doing is eating people, and once he manages to eat someone, he grows a little bit bigger. He initially showed some sympathy toward his creators (Gumball and Darwin), refusing to eat them while consuming others like the mailman and Anais. However, after eating Richard, he lost this sympathy and subsequently attacked both Gumball and Darwin as well.
