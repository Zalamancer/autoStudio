# Judith Fitzgerald

## Appearance
Judith Fitzgerald is presumably a shape-shifting fairy and has a similar appearance to Penny in season 2 and her younger daughter, Polly. She stands noticeably taller than her older daughter Penny and has smaller antlers. Her current outfit includes grey cowboy boots.

During her younger years, she wore a yellow dress and a blonde wig. Early storyboards depicted her shell as oval-shaped, similar to Penny's original design from season 1.

## Personality
Limited information exists about her character traits. However, she demonstrates extremely generous qualities, offering substantial aid including money, footwear, jewelry, and even organ donation when she perceived others needed assistance. She maintains an open-minded stance regarding her daughter's romantic relationship.

A darker aspect emerges in later episodes where she displays sadistic tendencies. She attempts intimidation tactics, including creating threatening imagery, to influence family decisions about personal choices.
