# Anais Watterson

## Appearance
Anais is a small pink rabbit with a white-colored tail who resembles her father Richard but with feminine characteristics and curved ears. She typically wears an orange dress with a white trim and white socks and is one of the only members of the Wattersons to wear footwear.

In early concepts, she was a dark pink rabbit with an elongated head and shorter limbs, wearing a yellow dress. She was initially planned as a three-year-old before becoming four. Season 2 introduced oval-shaped eyes and lighter pink coloring; Season 3 made her eyes permanently round. Her pajamas are pink footless pajamas with a white rabbit silhouette.

## Personality
Anais is by far the most intelligent member of her family and attends eighth grade despite being four years old. She demonstrates problem-solving abilities, cunning strategy, and extensive knowledge across subjects like physics and sewing.

Beyond her mother, Anais represents the family's most mature member, often functioning as an older sister by helping with homework and social guidance. She exhibits her mother's fiery temper, becoming rude, condescending, and occasionally violent when upset.

As a newborn, Anais displayed dangerous, manipulative tendencies when alone with her brothers, later maturing beyond this behavior. She remains childish despite her maturity, enjoys Daisy merchandise, and struggles with social interactions and friendship formation. She invents advanced devices, creates artwork, and demonstrates musical abilities.
