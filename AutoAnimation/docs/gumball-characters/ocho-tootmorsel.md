# Ocho Tootmorsel

## Appearance
Ocho is a small, black 8-bit spider. His design is either a tribute to the design of the alien ships in the arcade game Space Invaders or one of the Jelectros from Super Mario Bros. 3. The character underwent visual changes across seasons, becoming dark-grey in season 2, while his prototype featured blue coloring with smaller eyes and more 2D styling.

## Personality
Ocho displays contrasting personality traits. He can be nice and friendly when interacting with others that are nice to him, but becomes erratic and angry when provoked. His anger issues are so severe he'll attack others for insults. The character also exhibits a somewhat twisted sense of humor and has trust issues stemming from believing people only befriend him due to his famous uncle. Later seasons reveal he became bullied for his appearance, and his volatility stems from feelings of loneliness and being an outsider both in origin and appearance.
