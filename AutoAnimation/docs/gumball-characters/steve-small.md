# Steve Small

## Appearance
Mr. Small is a tall, fluffy creature with cloud-like characteristics, similar to Masami and Tobias. He wears a rainbow-patterned shirt, black bell-bottoms, and blue and yellow sandals. His body is covered in white fluff, particularly on his forearms, feet, and head, with a small ponytail. His eyes are round and colored lime green with red pupils.

In "The Painting," his interpretative dance outfit reveals his very bony and skinny structure on the inside. In "The Lie," he removes facial fluff, revealing his primate-like face.

As a junior high student, he had longer hair and wore a pink shirt with a yin yang symbol. Starting in season 2, his design changed slightly with added eyebrows and altered sandal designs. His voice also evolved from a calm southern accent in season 1 to a more stereotypical hippie voice in later seasons.

## Personality
Mr. Small serves as the guidance counselor but his methods are strange and somewhat ineffective. He loves helping students, using unconventional techniques like screaming and paint-based expression. He aspires to inspire children and feels guilt when believing he hasn't succeeded.

He is a hippie and a new ager. He enjoys meditation and maintains a large array of spiritual paraphernalia such as incense, healing crystals, and scented candles. He listens to mystic chants and enjoys sitar music.

In season 1, he displayed a temper, but became more mellow afterward. He is shown to be cowardly and weak. He believes conspiracy theories and uses unconventional "alternative medicine" approaches.
