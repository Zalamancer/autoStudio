# Alan Keane

## Appearance
Alan is a slightly deflated, teal-colored rubber balloon, whose face is drawn on with black permanent marker ink. His design evolved across seasons: from Season 2 onward, his eyes became slightly more round/oval-shaped and his coloring appeared brighter. In the early prototype, he had a nose and freckles creating a more childlike appearance, which were removed for the official series.

## Personality
Alan is characterized as having a "perfect" personality -- attractive, friendly, and positive. His high-pitched voice results from being helium-filled. He demonstrates selfless behavior and maintains optimism despite negative circumstances.

However, complexity emerges in later episodes. While his do-gooder nature annoys Gumball in "The Saint," the episode "The Vision" reveals darker dimensions: extremely manipulative, egotistical, and power-hungry inner thoughts. He exploits vulnerabilities and harbors intense hatred for sad people.

Despite his generally positive demeanor, Alan has limits. He displays anger when interrupted, falsely accused, or when personal boundaries are violated. "The Faith" establishes his significance: losing faith causes the world to lose color, underscoring his importance as a forgiving, understanding figure whose belief sustains Elmore's vibrancy.
