# Juke

## Appearance
Juke has a radio-like head and wears a brown shirt, blue jeans, and white sneakers. He is fairly short and has a heavy build, with short stubby legs and arms. His communication relies on beatboxing since nobody can understand this language, requiring hand gestures instead. A switch on the back of his head allows voice or music mode, though he cannot reach it himself. From Season 2 onward, he gained eyebrows, color-changing eyes reflecting moods, a darker shirt shade, and darker grey head buttons and top.

## Personality
Juke appears stoic, usually sporting a blank stare and being unintelligible, yet remains quite reserved and calm despite being very irritable. However, he may be extroverted -- he instantly communicates with Alan when he starts speaking his language and shows eagerness for conversation. His behavior may imply he's desperate for friends, particularly evident through his bond closely with Chris Morris. Additionally, Juke also speaks with an accent, most likely because he's an exchange student.
