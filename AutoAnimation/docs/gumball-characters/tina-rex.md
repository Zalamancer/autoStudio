# Tina Rex

## Appearance
Tina is a large and extremely fearsome Tyrannosaurus rex with thick, brownish-gray scales from head to tail, a huge mouth full of enormous, white carnivore teeth and very noticeable halitosis. In season 2, she underwent a redesign featuring a more brownish coloration with a realistic appearance. Her eyes became less slanted and more circular in shape.

## Personality
Tina serves as the resident bully of Elmore Junior High and is one of the meanest, largest, and strongest students. She regularly intimidates weaker students like Idaho and Anton, with Jamie Russo as her primary companion in bullying. Despite her aggressive exterior, she is a sensitive and somewhat insecure individual at heart who doesn't properly know how to express acts of generosity. Her bullying reportedly stems from others' failure to recognize her femininity, creating a self-perpetuating cycle where she behaves monstrously because she appears monstrous. Post-season one, Tina exhibits increased generosity, suggesting personal maturation. However, she remains prone to physical threats and violence when upset, as demonstrated in various episodes where she threatens harm to those who frustrate her.
