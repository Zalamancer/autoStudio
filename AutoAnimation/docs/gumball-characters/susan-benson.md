# Susan Benson

## Appearance
Susan has a mushroom top for a head. Her body is completely white, which matches the fact she is a mushroom. She is also shown to be as tall as her husband. She wears a pearl necklace, a red top, dark red pants, and orange pumps.

## Personality
She seems to be quite mature, as she was the one who noticed that there was no oil below the Robinsons' house, but raw sewage. She describes the Wattersons as "unbearable," and may dislike immature people as well.
