# Mayor of Elmore

## Appearance
The mayor of Elmore is a humanoid character. His hands, arms, and legs are small, and his head, having a blond wig and a somewhat wrinkled face, is disproportionately large. He wears a business suit with a badge attached to it.

## Personality
He possesses rather a selfish and mercenary personality, as shown in "The Nuisance," where he wants to get rid of the Wattersons and move them to Ohio, his motives being that they are bringing the prices of the neighborhood down. He also demonstrates dishonesty by appropriating ideas from others for personal gain, prioritizing financial interests over ethical considerations.
