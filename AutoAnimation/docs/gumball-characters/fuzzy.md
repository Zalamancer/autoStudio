# Fuzzy

## Appearance
Fuzzy is a Furby-like toy covered in pink and purple fur. He also has toufu ears with yellow holes, and big round blue eyes with purple eyelids and a red nose on a yellow face. Near the conclusion of "The BFFs," it is revealed that Fuzzy is actually an animatronic with a red eye after Darwin strikes him with a shovel.

## Personality
Fuzzy typically displays indifference toward Gumball and Darwin, though he shares a strong bond with Gumball. The character eventually exposes hidden jealousy about Gumball's friendship with Darwin, keeping him confined while seeking to be the sole best friend. Additionally, he demonstrates remarkable stamina during his journey from California to Minnesota.
