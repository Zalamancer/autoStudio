# Jackie Wilson

## Appearance
Jackie Wilson has chartreuse skin with hair that fades from light blue, to deep blue, to purple, to magenta. She wears a violet red strapless top, iris purple pants, lime green accessories, and a striped headband. Starting in Season 2, her shirt became sleeveless, eyebrows were added, and her shoes gained dark shading.

Not available. (The Trivia section notes she apparently loves to shop, as she is often seen with shopping bags in her appearances, but no formal Personality section exists.)
