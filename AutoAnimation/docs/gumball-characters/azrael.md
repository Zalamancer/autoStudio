# Azrael

## Appearance
Wearing a gray hoodie, gray cuffed pants, and gray shoes, Azrael is a ghost whose head, partially hidden by his hood, has hollow eyes and a prominent pointy "beak." His aesthetic draws inspiration from medieval plague doctor imagery. The hoodie displays a logo resembling The Neighbourhood's album design, while his pants feature distinctive cuffing at the ankles.

## Personality
Azrael is shown to be rather laid-back and relaxed. He maintains friendly, platonic connections with Carrie despite her relationship with Darwin, remaining unbothered by romantic entanglements -- though Gumball suspects otherwise.
