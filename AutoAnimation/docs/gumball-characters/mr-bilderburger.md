# Mr. Bilderburger

## Appearance
As the name suggests, Mr. Bilderburger is a sentient hamburger consisting of the usual hamburger fillings: cheese, onions, a pickle, and a singular patty, contained within a white Styrofoam to-go box. He has googly eyes with frowning eyebrows and a blue striped tie. His mouth flaps when he talks, similar to a hand puppet's.

## Personality
Mr. Bilderburger is an evil, ruthless, greedy, corporate businessman who takes enjoyment out of mankind's suffering. He demonstrates apathy toward consumer health and worker dignity, even forcing an employee to wear a dress to reduce labor costs. He sees everyone as dumb enough to fall for his traps of eating Joyful Burger food and also sees the lower class as beneath him. His miserly nature extends to his own workspace, which he keeps dark and poorly lit. According to Mr. Bilderburger, he is the way he is either because he is inherently evil or because he has a difficult and complicated relationship with his father, who's a corn dog.
