# Banana Joe

## Appearance
Banana Joe is an anthropomorphic yellow banana with googly eyes and a mouth made of clay, similar to Clayton's. He wears and treats his "peel" like a shirt -- he can remove it at will, and has several extras messily stuffed into his clothing drawer. The bottom end of his nude banana body is crafted to resemble buttocks. He also lacks feet, which, as revealed in "The Promise," is due to frostbite.

From Season 2 onward, his design evolved with added eyebrows and increased size, with horizontally-aligned equal-sized eyes and deeper red mouth interior. In "The Uploads," it is revealed that he can summon a second head at will.

## Personality
As the class clown, Banana Joe frequently attempts humor, though his jokes are perceived as annoying, embarrassing, or childish rather than humorous. He demonstrates dim-witted and clumsy tendencies.

Season 1 portrayed him as a bully with hurtful jokes, but starting with Season 2, his personality changes significantly, becoming a much more likable and friendly person while still continuing his antics in a more innocent fashion.

Additional traits include: distaste for girls, passion for creating videos for Elmore Stream-It, frequent nudity, and strong family values. His video uploads typically feature dancing, singing, or stunts that are frequently interrupted by his family members or his own incompetence.
