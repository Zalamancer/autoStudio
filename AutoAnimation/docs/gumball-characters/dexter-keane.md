# Dexter Keane

## Appearance
Dexter, like his son and his wife, is a rubber balloon, but is orange (red in a few episodes). He wears glasses and has a mustache. In "The Bus," he is red and has a different mustache. In "The Fuss," he is seen in formal "attire;" he has slicked down sideburns.
