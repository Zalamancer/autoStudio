# Bobert 6B

## Appearance
Bobert is a sleek, white robot with clamps for hands and a speaker for a mouth, which glows green when he talks. His head features a large blue eye capable of recording video and sound, with a memory-clearing button on his left ear.

The character can transform into a large mechanical form called "combat mode" or "Mega-Bobert," equipped with Gatling guns, rocket launchers, shoulder-mounted weapons, and a chest cannon. In Season 2, Bobert received a redesign that removed a black strip near his eye, thickened the eye rim, made his pupil smaller, altered his arm coloring to more gray, changed his speakers to red, and enlarged his overall body proportions.

## Personality
As a robot, Bobert struggles to process human emotions but remains highly analytical. He occasionally envies organic classmates and has attempted to become human, once stealing Gumball's identity. His logical worldview sometimes leads to amoral behavior, nearly endangering humanity in one episode.

Despite being called a nerd and joining the Reject Club, Bobert is not entirely emotionless. He deeply values friendships with Gumball and Darwin and experiences love, as shown when he falls for a vacuum cleaner. He demonstrates humor and gradually becomes more comfortable with his robotic nature over time, eventually preferring acceptance for who he is rather than attempting extreme transformations to fit in.
