# Polly Fitzgerald

## Appearance
Like the rest of her family, underneath her shell, she is a shape-shifting fairy. Her appearance is similar to that of her older sister: antlers on her head, and holes in her shell for her eyes and limbs. Unlike her parents or sister (up to the events of "The Shell"), she does not wear shoes or socks, and her and her parents' shape-shifting fairy forms were never seen, as they never broke out of their shells. In the storyboard for "The Date," her shell was oval-shaped like her sister's and father's. She also wore a skirt and socks.
