# Porky Cassidy

## Appearance
Like Hot Dog Guy and Earl, the character is a large anthropomorphic hot dog, with very thin arms and legs. He is always seen wearing a pair of dark sunglasses.

## Personality
In "The Castle," Porky appears to be quite easy-going, as he seems to only want to stay in the Wattersons' house to make a point: taking off his buns and acting as though this move is a shocking one, going against social norms, despite the buns only covering his back. He acknowledges that this is not very logical, but seems to be happy anyway.
