# Pantsbully

## Appearance
In season 1, Pantsbully is a bright blue, lineless humanoid with a prominent unibrow and big pink lips. He has a bean-shaped body, usually clad in his light blue underpants and black boots. He also wears a black hat with a blue skull on it. From season 2 onwards, he has a much simpler design and is a lighter shade of blue -- the most noticeable change being that he now has two normal eyebrows instead of a unibrow. He then has a pear-shaped body, instead of staying on his bean-shaped figure from the first season.

## Personality
Pantsbully seems to have many different occupations around Elmore, similar to other recurring minor characters. He has worked as a construction worker, sewer worker, and street vendor across different episodes. In "The Genius," he appeared for a split second on a website called "Being a Genius for Fools," implying that he may not be very intelligent.
