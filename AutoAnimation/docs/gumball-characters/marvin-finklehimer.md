# Marvin Finklehimer

## Appearance
Marvin Finklehimer is a short, round, and red bean-like figure. He wears a small brown hat and walks with a cane. He has only three teeth and overly large eyes with purple pupils. His face is wrinkled due to age, and his limbs appear shaky and frail.

## Personality
Marvin displays energetic behavior despite his age, though he can also be quite frail. He is stubborn at times. As a senior citizen, he feels neglected due to technology's impact on intergenerational communication. Marvin is quite flirtatious, as he goes on a date with the hexagon lady in "The Watch" and flirts with her in "The Tag." He demonstrates this trait by offering dates to other characters despite appearing weak moments before.
