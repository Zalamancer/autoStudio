# Carmen Verde

## Appearance
Carmen is an anthropomorphic green cactus, with thin black lines for arms and legs. Her facial features include black eyes and mouth with a green tongue. By Season 2, her cactus arms moved closer to her head, outlines became thicker, and her face repositioned to the middle of her body with rounder eyes replacing oval ones. Her early prototype featured a single eye with more prominent spikes.

## Personality
Carmen functions as a leader among her peers, characterized as one of the smartest and most capable people in the school. She maintains high personal standards, becoming extremely upset when she fails at something. She dedicates considerable time to academics and reading, with a serious, focused demeanor that sets her apart from classmates. Her critical nature can be irritating to others.

Her relationship with Alan involves romantic feelings, though this connection faces complications due to the inherent incompatibility between cactus spines and balloons. The episode "The Best" reveals her transformation: she was formerly a troublemaker at Franklin Junior High (expelled for poor grades, vandalism, and disrespect toward authority), but reformed significantly upon transferring to Elmore Junior High.
