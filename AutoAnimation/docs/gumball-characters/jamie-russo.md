# Jamie Russo

## Appearance
Jamie appears to be a hybrid creature combining traits from multiple animal species. She possesses an ox's horns, teeth, and hooves; a lion's tail and orange coloring; frog-like skin coloring; and features from various other animals. She has bright orange hair covering most of her head, small curved horns, greenish-white skin, stocky limbs, a mint green shirt, and dark green pants. From Season 2 onward, her skin became darker green, making her shirt more visible. Beginning in Season 3, her eyes are fully covered by hair, though they appear when she experiences surprise or deep thought.

## Personality
Jamie is a tomboy and one of the bullies of Elmore Junior High. She is tough, crazy, sadistic, and mean-spirited, with her best friend Tina, showing little remorse for bullying weaker students. She struggles to understand genuine emotion and attempted to force Darwin and Gumball into relationships, believing love could be forced rather than earned. She despises authority figures and her younger classmates after being held back by Miss Simian. However, she demonstrated a gentler side when befriending Anais in "The Buddy," working together cooperatively. Jamie exhibits low intelligence, using nonexistent words and freezing while thinking, though she occasionally demonstrates cleverness during specific situations.
