# Fuzzball

## Appearance
Fuzzball is, as their name implies, a black fuzzball made up of scribbles. They have stubby arms and long legs. The only clothes they seem to wear are outlined, white sneakers, and in earlier episodes they simply wore nothing. On the top of their head appears to be a knot of some sort, which is made of the same scribble the rest of their body is made up of. In "The Extras," they are rounder and have a mouth, and their sneakers are only outlines. In "The App," their mouth has been redesigned to be more "scribble-like."
