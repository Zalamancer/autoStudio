# Ribbit

## Appearance
Ribbit is an aqua-blue frog who looks very similar to Darwin, despite being a frog. He wears purple shorts and is barefoot. He has two toes on each foot and two fingers on each hand.

## Personality
Akin to the relationship between Gumball and Chi Chi, Ribbit emulates everything that Darwin says and does. As shown in "The Copycats," the two share the same characterization as well, as when he is fighting against Darwin, they both flinch, then apologize upon hitting one another.
