# Carrie Krueger

## Appearance
In Season 1, Carrie is a white, translucent ghost with a ghostly tail in place of her legs and a hairclip that resembles a skull with wings. Her arms rest in front of her body with hair covering her left eye. She features magenta and cyan lining around her, making her seem like a 3D anaglyph image, with sharp, pointed teeth and black eyebrows when visible.

Season 2 brought animation style changes: her head becomes slightly more rectangular, and she now has a shoulder on each side of her body, which her arms now hang from. She also becomes a darker color, and her outline gets thicker, like the other 2D characters on the show with outlines.

## Personality
Carrie embodies a stereotypical emo aesthetic while remaining socially versatile, described as a social floater who will talk to anyone. Though she claims pain is the only emotion she can feel, she demonstrates compassion, concern, and even romantic feelings -- she blushes when Darwin kisses her.

Her character reveals depth: she found emotional resonance in unexpected places and shows selfless tendencies, willingly sacrificing herself for others' safety. Initially shy around Darwin, she becomes more honest about her feelings over time, ultimately embracing their relationship.
