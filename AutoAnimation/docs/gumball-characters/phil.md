# Phil

## Appearance
In "The News" and "The Brain," he is seen only in front of a green screen, thus he blends in with whatever background is behind him. His facial features include a red bulbous nose, large black glasses, brown combover, and bushy mustache. His outfit consists of a suit, tie, white gloves, pants, and brown shoes.

When appearing away from the green screen in "The Founder," Phil has pale yellow skin, a grey suit and pants, and a pink tie. He no longer wears gloves and sports an identity card on his lapel.

## Personality
In early episodes, Phil displays a chipper and amiable disposition, though this changes when interrupted. In "The Founder," he becomes a deadpan character who remains calm during chaos. This game ignites emotion in him when he realizes that dancing is what he wants to do with his life, though he ultimately returns to his emotionless demeanor when confronted with the building's collapse.
