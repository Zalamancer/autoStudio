# Jodie Mallard

## Appearance
Jodie is a short orange duck with orange cheeks. She is somewhere between Gumball and Anais in height. She has short black hair and does not wear any clothes.

## Personality
She is very anti-social and nervous, and she speaks in a laconic manner. Anais' social awkwardness caused her more trouble, forming a parasite of both of them.
