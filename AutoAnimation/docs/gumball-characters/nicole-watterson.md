# Nicole Watterson

## Appearance
Nicole closely resembles her eldest son Gumball, being a cat with light cerulean fur positioned between Gumball and Richard in height. Her head mirrors Gumball's design but with shorter whiskers, a pink nose, and visible eyelashes. She typically wears a short-sleeve collared white shirt with an orange, yellow, and green striped button representing the Rainbow Factory, paired with a light gray pleated skirt, usually barefoot like her family.

Her Season 2 redesign showed her shirt tucked in, a slimmer body with wider hips, rounder mouth and head, disappearing shadows, and shorter whiskers. From Season 3 onward, her eyes became permanently round. She is depicted in sleepwear as pink and white pajamas or a yellow and white bathrobe with towels while preparing for bed or morning routines.

Historical appearances include 1990s-style fashion during Gumball's toddler years, a white karate uniform with red belt in early adolescence, and various teenage outfits. A prom flashback shows her in a short pink dress with the skirt having three frilly layers with permed hair.

## Personality
Nicole demonstrates profound family devotion, willing to do anything for her loved ones' comfort and safety. As the family's backbone, she handles household responsibilities and works extended hours at Rainbow Factory as the sole employed member.

However, she suffers from a tremendous amount of emotional stress due to her many duties. She is quite strict and a demanding mother with extremely nasty temper issues when upset, even attending anger management classes. Despite these challenges, she remains a good mother and person, very supportive, kindhearted, and forgiving with genuine family love.

Nicole demonstrates resourcefulness, strong penny-pinching abilities, and couponing skills learned from her family. She dislikes losing but avoids fighting those with close relationships unless they threaten her family or employment.
