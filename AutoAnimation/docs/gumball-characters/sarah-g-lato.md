# Sarah G. Lato

## Appearance
Sarah is an anthropomorphic ice cream cone with a yellow ice cream portion and orange cone with brown stripes. She has light orange cheeks, thin limbs matching her head color, and red lips. Notably, she possesses yellow irises, making her one of few characters with this eye color.

## Personality
Initially soft-spoken with minimal dialogue, Sarah later demonstrates a forceful personality when pursuing goals. She exhibits obsessive tendencies, particularly regarding Gumball and Darwin, whom she views as her only friends. She carries a tape recorder playing laugh tracks and sitcom music, reflecting her perception of life as entertainment. Despite therapeutic sessions with Mr. Small for her obsessive-compulsive behaviors, Sarah continues struggling with compulsion. However, she possesses practical judgment and becomes irritated by Gumball's foolishness. She displays a temper when frustrated and has demonstrated knowledge of superheroes, sitcom tropes, anime, and fictional character relationships ("ships") throughout various episodes.
