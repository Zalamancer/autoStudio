# Saturn

## Appearance
Saturn resembles his real-life counterpart, albeit with a face. He is also a different color: at first, he was pea-green and purple, but was changed in later seasons to be red, peach, and blue. His ring system was also made thicker and more opaque, more closely resembling real-life Saturn's rings.
