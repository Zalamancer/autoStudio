# Felicity Parham

## Appearance
Felicity Parham is an orange humanoid with blue lips. Her figure is a simple shape, consisting of an abnormally large head with hair of the same color as her skin, noodle-like arms, normal legs, and a bulbous body. She usually wears no clothes.

## Personality
Felicity displays extreme snobbiness and discourages others without remorse, viewing herself as superior. She maintains a rivalry with Nicole Watterson due to her sophisticated pretenses. Additionally, she is obstinate and easily annoyed. Her parenting style is overprotective and overbearing. She opposes various influences on her son Billy, including video games, rock music, long hair, and vaccinations, believing these harm him negatively. The character demonstrates instability, as evidenced by episodes where she experiences violent outbursts. Witnessing the Donut Cop ignoring Gumball and Darwin's jaywalking angers her enough to go on a rampage, and later holding a police chief hostage. Her dignified persona proves fragile, collapsing under minor frustrations into erratic behavior.
