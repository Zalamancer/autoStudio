# Mr. Gruber

## Appearance
He is a bald eagle dressed in a standard mailman uniform with a gold belt to go along with it. The top of his head is red, as well as his eyes, and he has seven blond hair strands of different sizes. He also has thick black eyebrows.

## Personality
He exhibits strict, rule-focused behavior as a delivery professional. He demonstrates capacity for reasoning, offering Richard alternatives like payment or depot pickup. However, his patience depletes when Richard neglects to choose, leading him to devise schemes involving deception -- disguising himself as an ice cream vendor and pretending to be Richard's hospitalized wife. He makes multiple failed attempts to prevent Richard's package delivery while following postal regulations. Notably, he frequently performs incorrect mime gestures despite claiming to love mime. He shows familial care, distinguishing between laughing at his plan versus his brother's medical emergency.
