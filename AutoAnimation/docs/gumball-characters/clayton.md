# Clayton

## Appearance
Clayton is a living ball made of red clay, with big purple clay lips, and eyes made from white and black clay. In season 2, his design was updated with thick eyebrows, his clay is more fixed, and the inside of his mouth is pink.

## Personality
Clayton is characterized by his tendency to tell exaggerated or false stories to impress others. He struggles with loneliness and uses deception to gain friendships, as shown in episodes like "The Skull" and "The Move." Clayton reveals that he finds himself boring and socially awkward and that is why he has a tendency to lie often. He feels compelled to change who he is in order to find companionship, though he eventually learns that being himself is acceptable.
