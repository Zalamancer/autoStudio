# Richard Watterson

## Appearance
Richard shares similarities with his daughter Anais as both are pink rabbits, though he is significantly larger and overweight. He has long straight ears, a darker pink nose, three black whiskers on each side, and long eyelashes. Despite being unemployed, he consistently wears a white dress shirt tucked into light brown pants with a dark grey clip-on tie. He goes barefoot like other family members.

His appearance evolved across seasons. In Season 2, his belly shadow lightened, his body became rounder, and his shirt sleeves extended fully. His tongue changed from red to light pink. From Season 3 onward, his eyes became permanently round.

As a young adult, he wore a red-and-black plaid shirt with white sleeves, a black t-shirt with a yellow smiley face on it, and ripped jeans. During adolescence, he had freckles and wore a yellow jacket over a green sweatshirt. In his teenage years, he developed acne and wore a black t-shirt resembling a Motorhead logo with a flannel shirt tied around his waist.

## Personality
Richard serves as the father figure but lacks responsibility and common sense. He is childlike, lazy, and not a good role model, though he genuinely loves his family and means well.

He is obsessed with eating, particularly sausages, leading to his overweight condition. His childish behaviors include believing in ghosts and Santa Claus, and he exhibits hypocritical humor -- enjoying pranks on others but hating when pranked himself.

His personality stems from overprotective parenting by his mother. Despite his incompetency, he occasionally offers sound advice and demonstrates a simple-minded good nature that helps uplift his family. He shows effeminate tendencies and genuinely cares about helping his loved ones.
