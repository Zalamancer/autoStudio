# Daniel Senicourt

## Appearance
Daniel is a short and pudgy snowshoe cat with cream-white fur, brown ears, and brown eye circles. He typically wears a white collared shirt with rolled sleeves, dark blue tie, gray pants, and brown loafers, maintaining a grumpy expression. In later episodes, he appears more elderly with faded fur, wrinkles, and a hunched posture.

## Personality
Daniel is short-tempered and grumpy, sharing his daughter Nicole's tendency toward anger and violence. He was notably unusually strong and disapproved of Nicole's relationship with Richard. His stressful parenting approach focused on high demands and expectations. However, he later demonstrates emotional depth, keeping a picture of young Nicole and expressing sadness about their estrangement. He eventually reconciles with Nicole, showing he wanted to start over despite their troubled history.
