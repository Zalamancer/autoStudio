# Chimera

## Appearance
He is a combination of a great white shark's head with black hair, multiple building blocks for a body, a muscular action figure right arm with a spike bracelet, a left arm with a buzz saw attached to the end of it, a human right leg, a scaly left leg, and a crocodile's tail. The character can also detach and reassemble his body parts due to his toy-like construction.

## Personality
Despite his frightening appearance, the chimera demonstrates kindness and social awkwardness. The chimera also has a more bold and courageous side, which is shown when he goes as far as threatening to blow himself up when the police try to arrest him, displaying selflessness. He connects well with Anais through shared social difficulty and notably enjoys consuming hair.
