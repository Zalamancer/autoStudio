# Joanna Watterson (Granny Jojo)

## Appearance
Joanna is a pink rabbit with a short stature, cat-eye glasses with purple lenses, and wrinkled features typical of an elderly woman. She speaks with a New Jersey accent.

In season 1, she wore a beige polka-dotted dress with a white collar and sleeve cuffs and white socks with black shoes, with straight ears resembling Richard's and three whiskers per cheek.

From season 2 onward, her design changed to show a plain beige dress with white sleeve cuffs, shorter stature, extra facial wrinkles, rounder head, wider hips, and ears resembling Anais'. "The Man" reveals she wears a girdle that significantly shapes her appearance.

In younger flashbacks from "The Choices" and "The Cycle," she wore a yellow collared dress with a light brown vest and displayed fewer wrinkles and less sagging skin.

## Personality
Joanna possesses a grouchy and somewhat lazy personality. She spends considerable time watching TV and is very dedicated to receiving kisses from her grandchildren. She shows little respect for Darwin and Gumball, calling them "fish kid" and "the blue one," though she addresses Anais by name.

Her parenting approach involves sheltering children, as she stated: "A good mother prevents the incident before it happens." This controlling style significantly impacted Richard's development.

She is extremely possessive and jealous regarding her husband Louie and generally dislikes her daughter-in-law Nicole. Despite her unhappiness, she became less downhearted after beginning to date Louie in season 3.
