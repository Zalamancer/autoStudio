# Leonard Daniels

## Appearance
He is a yellow-colored lightbulb man with a few hairs sticking out of his bald head. He wears a light blue shirt, along with a red tie. He also wears white underwear instead of wearing pants. He has a black mustache, and a black mole, as well.

## Personality
Leonard is very friendly, and willing to help out complete strangers without too many qualms. He demonstrates patience despite mistreatment, though he tends to be oblivious, making him unpopular in certain situations. A creepier side of his personality is implied in one episode where he engages in voyeuristic behavior.
