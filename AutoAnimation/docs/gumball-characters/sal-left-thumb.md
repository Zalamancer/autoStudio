# Sal Left Thumb

## Appearance
Sal was originally designed as a student at Elmore Junior High with a more realistic appearance, lacking both a bandanna and arms. In his final design, Sal resembles an over-sized, sentient fingerprint wearing a domino mask. He has very long and skinny limbs that are small relative to his body, and can flatten himself to appear as a regular fingerprint, which helps him evade capture.

## Personality
Sal is a dangerous and infamous criminal with an abrasive nature who threatens anyone interfering with his activities. He taunts victims with sarcasm and calls people "suckers" when they fall for his tricks. Despite his current criminal ways, he aspired to be a singer as a young person but was unsuccessful, leading him to pursue crime instead.
