# Leslie

## Appearance
Leslie is a pink flower who stays grounded in a blue flowerpot with a thin green stem, two long leaves serving as arms, and green stubby roots functioning as feet. His mouth interior is purple with a red tongue. Starting in Season 2, he gained eyebrows like other redesigned characters. His eye color varies across episodes -- sometimes blue, sometimes green. In the early prototype, his petals were baby-blue. A significant revelation occurs in "The Awareness" when his pink petals fall off, exposing his true dandelion form.

## Personality
Leslie exhibits a gentle nature combined with occasional hyperactivity and creepiness. He is intelligent and wise, notably effeminate in manner and voice, and frequently associates with the Treehouse Girls while dispensing relationship advice. His feminine presentation causes many to mistake him for female. He demonstrates musical talent across multiple instruments and possesses a strong interest in fashion and image. His plant biology significantly shapes his personality -- he is a passionate carnivore who finds fruit and vegetable consumption morally objectionable, and holds limited understanding of animal life, including misconceptions about daily milk requirements for mammals.
