# Goblin

## Appearance
The goblin is a green round goblin. He wears a worn-out bike vest and black pants. His skin changes color in almost every one of his appearances. In "The Finale," his skin displays a brighter shade of green, whereas in "The Money," his skin appears whitish-green. A similarly designed character, the office goblin, makes his first appearance in "The Boss" as an employee of Chanax Inc. before assuming the role of CEO. The most notable difference between the two is their skin color and ear line shape.
