# Bulldog

## Appearance
He is a pink bulldog wearing an orange and brown varsity jacket with a large E on the left of it. On his head is a beret. In "The Nuisance," he gets a minor redesign: his legs are much shorter, meaning that when he sits on a chair, his legs stick out in front of him, rather than his feet touching the floor as he had previously. Additionally, he has what appears to be a pixelated filter over him.
