# Tobias Wilson

## Appearance
Tobias is a multi-colored, fluffy, cloud-like humanoid creature with three fingers on each hand. His coloring includes red for most of his body, light orange on the lower portion, and sky-blue on top. He wears a green and white headband along with red and white shoes and has blue lips.

Design changes occurred in Season 2. Notably, the shadow that he has on his arms and legs disappears and he also acquires eyebrows. Additionally, his pupils and outline became darker.

## Personality
Tobias exhibits significant arrogance and self-aggrandizement. He believes that he is a heavily built jock, but in reality, he is not, yet remains obsessed with sports. His inflated ego makes him more confident in his abilities to court Penny than Gumball is.

The character displays bullying tendencies and narcissism. He demonstrates behavior such as insulting peers and finding humor in others' misfortune. Because of Tobias's narcissism, he does not understand true friendship.

Romantically, Tobias seems to be quite the womanizer. He pursues numerous girls despite their rejection, continuing in hopes of one day having someone further inflate his ego. His background indicates wealth: Tobias comes from a wealthier household than most of his classmates.
