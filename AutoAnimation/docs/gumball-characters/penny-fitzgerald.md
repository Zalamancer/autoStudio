# Penny Fitzgerald

## Appearance
Penny's design evolved significantly throughout the series. Initially appearing as an anthropomorphic antlered peanut with a light brown shell and tan antlers, she wore orange and tan sneakers with white socks in early seasons. Her shell design changed from Season 1's straight, round shape to Season 2's curved design resembling her mother and sister.

The pivotal episode "The Shell" revealed her true form: a shape-shifting, fairy-like creature capable of transforming based on emotions. Her actual body features a round head with a slim body with yellow, orange, and pale pink coloration. She possesses white eyelashes, orange-brown pupils, and an orange mouth. The character generates a dim glow, giving her skin a slightly reflective appearance, and has functional wings enabling hovering and flight.

## Personality
Penny demonstrates consistent kindness and integrity, actively supporting friends and resisting peer pressure. She exhibits strong selflessness, willing to sacrifice her relationship with Gumball in order to make sure Darwin was happy in "The Bros."

However, she proves emotionally sensitive, easily hurt by misunderstandings. When her true form was revealed, she became very self-conscious and shy, initially fleeing society. She uses occasional sarcasm, particularly toward Tobias's romantic advances.

Despite her friendly nature, Penny can become harsh when upset or motivated, resorting to threats or humiliation to achieve goals. She demonstrates capability for extravagant and reckless behavior comparable to Gumball, as shown in her elaborate prom proposal attempts.
