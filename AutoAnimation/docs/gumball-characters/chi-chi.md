# Chi Chi

## Appearance
Chi Chi is a pale cream-colored goat whose appearance is very similar to Gumball's. He wears a light grayish-brown shirt with dark brown shorts, with red and white sneakers as footwear. He has two dark brown horns, a fluffy tail, and a tuft of hair on his head. He also has pink cheeks and an orange nose.

## Personality
Chi Chi is portrayed as having the exact same personality as Gumball, with a tendency to repeat his words in the exact same way and with the same tonation (as he is a copycat).
