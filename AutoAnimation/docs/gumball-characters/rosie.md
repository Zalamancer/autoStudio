# Rosie

## Appearance
Rosie is a small, plump rabbit that speaks in a cute high-pitched voice, and has white fur. She wears a daisy on her head, and there is a big heart in the middle of her chest. Starting from Season 2, the heart on Rosie's chest becomes bigger and a lighter shade of pink, and the tuft of hair on Rosie's forehead disappears.

## Personality
Rosie seems to be kind and sweet on the outside, but on the inside, she has a greedy personality, such as when she charged Gumball an obscene amount of cash to borrow her tricycle in "The Third."
