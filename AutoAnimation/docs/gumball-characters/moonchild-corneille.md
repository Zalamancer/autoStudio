# Moonchild Corneille

## Appearance
He is a lime green humanoid frog who wears a yellow dress shirt, khaki pants, and brown shoes. He has large pale yellow eyes protruding upward away from his head. He is covered by a filter that makes him resemble pixel art, though this pixelated filter disappears when he appears in photos or cameos.

## Personality
Mr. Corneille does not exercise adequately and relaxes all the time in his class. He has a weak body with withered muscles and becomes extremely intimidated by strong opponents. He claims his inactivity is to mentally strengthen his strategic mindset rather than weakening him. He shows passion for battling against intimidating deeds, or alternatively, fighting from the heart. He is generally laid-back and rarely distressed. "The Inquisition" reveals he did eight years hard labor prior to the series, suggesting a darker side to his personality.
