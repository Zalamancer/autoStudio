# Hot Dog Guy

## Appearance
Hot Dog Guy is a large anthropomorphic hot dog in a bun, with very thin arms and legs. He consistently wears dark sunglasses. Starting in "The Hug," he added a mustard-colored wig to conceal a birth scar on his head. Prior to "The Extras," the character lacked a visible mouth.

## Personality
The character struggles with uncomfortable social situations, particularly with Gumball, to the point where they've suppressed awkward shared memories. While somewhat composed, he avoids stepping outside his comfort zone -- demonstrated when he wore swimming trunks rather than showering naked alongside Gumball in "The Cringe." Beyond his interactions with Gumball, Hot Dog Guy demonstrates friendliness through frequent waves and conversations with various students throughout the school.
