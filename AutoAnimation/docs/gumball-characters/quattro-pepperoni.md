# Quattro Pepperoni

## Appearance
Quattro is an anthropomorphic pepperoni pizza (it being mostly his head) with a body attached. He wears a steel blue job outfit, along with a sailor's cap that levitates above his head.

## Personality
Quattro demonstrates strong family devotion, protecting his wife by attacking those who disrespect her. His primary conflict-resolution method involves violence, as shown when he attacks various characters. However, he displays generosity by giving Gumball and Darwin $20 for delivering a pizza, in spite of the fact that this is almost twice as much as he needed to pay.

In later episodes, he is portrayed as a caring father and husband with radical anarchistic beliefs. He despises the government, in large part due to their laws on homeschooling resulting in his son's speech impediment. Notably, he and his wife are largely ignorant of many things considered common knowledge, such as the internet or the idea of the Earth being round.
