# Lucy Simian

## Appearance
Miss Simian is a very old baboon. She has dark grey fur, a homely primate face, with grey hair (and a lighter-colored patch in the shape of a stylized skull). She wears a pale brownish-buff polka-dotted dress with notably long arms.

In Season 2, her design shifted significantly: her dress is pink with no dots and pink outlines, the wrinkles on her lips are gone, and her pelvis is not visible. Her hair now features white coloring resembling a mountain shape, and she displays white eyes with greenish irises. Later episodes show minor refinements to her model, including sunken eyeballs and eventually blue irises.

Miss Simian does not visibly age, as she has kept the same appearance since Nicole's childhood, attributable to her extreme age and Stone Age teaching tenure.

## Personality
Miss Simian exhibits a consistently grumpy demeanor with apparent indifference toward students, particularly Gumball, who she hates with a passion. She demonstrates vicious and sadistic tendencies, deriving pleasure from pop quizzes and deception. Her behavior stems partly from thousands of years in an apparently despised profession.

However, deeper characterization emerges: In "The Teacher," it is revealed that despite her usual attitude toward them, she does genuinely care about her students and their education. She prioritizes preventing student failure and views teaching as the most beautiful job in the world as she is helping to shape the future of her students.
