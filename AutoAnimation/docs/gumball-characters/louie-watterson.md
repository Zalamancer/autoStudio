# Louie Watterson

## Appearance
Louie is a black mouse featuring white paws, large circular ears, and a prominent dark gray nose that shifts when he speaks. Age lines surround his eyes, and his ears possess a textured quality reminiscent of chalkboards.

Starting with "The Man," his design changed significantly: his nose became black, his body enlarged, his mouth vanished, outlines and limbs thinned, his head connected directly to his torso, ear markings disappeared, wrinkles rounded out, ears shrank, and feet straightened. He was likely redesigned to make him easier to animate, as he would become a recurring character thereafter.

## Personality
Louie demonstrates remarkable patience and composure. In "The Man," he maintains tranquility while Richard shouts about him dating Jojo, attempting resolution through calm conversation while displaying exceptional self-control. He shows genuine kindness toward his stepgrandchildren, hoping to become an accepted family member and offering them financial gifts.

However, "The Signature" and "The Catfish" reveal his boundaries. He competes with Richard to pursue his marriage plans and disciplines Gumball and Darwin when their interference causes marital conflict. Louie is notably talkative and romantic, expressing flowery sentiments about Jojo. Despite deep affection for his wife, he disapproves of her demanding nature, particularly her jealousy that prevents him from associating with other women and old friends.
