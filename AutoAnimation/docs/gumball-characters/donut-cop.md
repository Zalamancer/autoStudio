# Donut Cop

## Appearance
The Donut Cop is a giant, pink donut who wears an officer's hat, star-shaped badge, and blue uniform. He has a large hole through his body that functions as his mouth, featuring large, flat teeth resembling popsicle sticks. Notably, he has pronounced eyelashes typically associated with female characters.

## Personality
The Donut Cop often intends to enforce the law but is extremely oblivious towards most criminal activity. He relies heavily on manuals and can become overly committed to proving points. He gets carried away with his ideas and may join in on chaos himself.

A significant quirk is that his mouth goes through his body, causing anything eaten to fall through him onto the ground behind him, leaving him perpetually hungry. His favorite foods are coffee and donuts, described as ironic given his own donut nature.
