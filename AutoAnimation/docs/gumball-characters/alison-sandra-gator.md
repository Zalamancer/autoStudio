# Alison Sandra Gator

## Appearance
Alison Sandra Gator is an elderly, pale-blue scaled crocodile. She wears a green dress with a matching beanie. She has white hair, and yellow eyes with black pupils. Her eyes, lips, arms, and legs are all very wrinkly. Rocky notes she possesses eighty-seven teeth and a particularly wide posterior measuring five feet across.

## Personality
She demonstrates aggressive tendencies when obstructed, as illustrated during her confrontation with Gumball over discounted poultry. She exhibits loneliness, readily accepting what she mistakes for a romantic proposal from Rocky. She maintains strong attachment to her pet cat Martin, whom she regards as human rather than feline.
