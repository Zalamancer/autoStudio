# Jeff Benson

## Appearance
Jeff has a large corncob (with the kernels) for a body, it having limbs sprouting out of it. He wears the leaf of the cob like a shirt, and does not have a neck. He also wears a pair of brown shoes and a belt.

## Personality
Jeff demonstrates generosity but can join angry mobs when deceived. In later episodes, he displays an awkward personality, often making embarrassing mistakes like mispronouncing words, singing off-cue, and getting stuck in elevators.
