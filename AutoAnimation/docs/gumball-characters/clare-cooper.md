# Clare Cooper

## Appearance
She is a grey-skinned, glittery green-haired humanoid whose bangs are styled to cover her right eye. She wears a hot pink jacket with yellow lines much like sprinkles, complete with a white shirt underneath it. She also wears black pants and yellow boots with bunched-up white socks.

## Personality
Clare demonstrates pessimistic tendencies stemming from her family's financial circumstances. She initially rejected assistance from Gumball and Darwin, doubting her prospects for a positive outcome. However, she genuinely values her friendships, particularly with Wilson. The character expresses herself through poetic and metaphorical language reflecting her emotional struggles. Additionally, she exhibits some rudeness, particularly by sighing at Anais's intellectual contributions.
