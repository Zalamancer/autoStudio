# Martha Miller

## Appearance
Mrs. Miller is an anthropomorphic cupcake. Her height is inconsistent, because in her first appearance, she is around the same height as Gumball, but in her second appearance, her legs are very long, and she is much taller than him.

Her head is a light shade of purple fading to blue, and a small amount of white sprinkles dot her head. Her body is a light shade of pink in season 1, while in her season 2 re-design, her head is pink, she has rainbow sprinkles, her body is white, and, like many characters, she gains eyebrows. However, her eyebrows are unique in that they are simply two of her sprinkles which move and contort to show emotion.

## Personality
Mrs. Miller seems to dislike children and her job, as she says most things in an indifferent and sarcastic voice. However, the fact that she says everything in a sarcastic tone means that it is hard to discern whether she is being sarcastic or serious. She also seems to smile rarely, reflecting her deadpan and blase attitude.
