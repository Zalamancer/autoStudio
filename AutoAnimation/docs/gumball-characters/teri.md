# Teri

## Appearance
Teri is a slightly crumpled paper cutout of a bear whose design resembles school doodles cut from folder paper. She typically wears a blazer with three permanent blush lines on her cheeks.

In Season 2, she gained eyebrows. "The Origins: Part Two" depicts her as smaller with crudely drawn features. Her early reel prototype appeared male in underwear, while an earlier final design showed her as plain white before receiving a dress.

## Personality
Teri is characterized as a hypochondriac whose condition deeply influences her behavior. She frequently imagines contracting multiple diseases based on minimal evidence, particularly evident in episodes like "The Virus" and "The Allergy."

Her hypochondria connects with severe mysophobia -- she constantly disinfects surroundings and people, even friends. This obsession with microscopic threats has made her academically strong in studying disease prevention.

Despite these anxieties, when she overcomes her fears, Teri is one of the nicer characters on the show, willing to befriend everyone that gives her a chance. Her self-absorption coexists with genuine kindness toward others.
