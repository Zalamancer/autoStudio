# Masami Yoshida

## Appearance
Masami is a small, white cloud with hollowed-out holes for her eyes and mouth possessing retractable arms. She can extend long retractable legs adorned with rainbow toe socks, as shown in "The Gift." When experiencing negative emotions like sadness or anger, her appearance changes -- she turns gray and stormy.

Her human form (appearing in "The Inquisition") resembles a Japanese schoolgirl wearing a white collared shirt, black tie, skirt, and shoes with dark gray socks. She has gray eyes, shoulder-length black hair, and light pinkish-orange freckles.

## Personality
Masami is characterized as somewhat snobbish due to her family's wealth and ownership of the Rainbow Factory. She displays a smug sense of superiority to her fellow students, frequently taking the chance to show off. She becomes enraged and destructive if she does not get her way.

The character exhibits narcissistic tendencies, taking great pride in her appearance -- notably refusing to enter a swimming pool to prevent her "hair" from getting wet. She struggles with criticism; when angered, she transforms into storms and uses lightning against those who annoy her.

However, she evolves throughout the series, becoming more respectable, friendly, and amiable, while still retaining her arrogance and smugness to a lesser degree, as noted in later seasons.
