# Rob

## Appearance
Rob was initially a tall cyclops with a very slim, blue body and a single large eye located in the center of his face, featuring a magenta iris. He had long limbs, pink hands, and wore red shorts with an orange-sleeved yellow shirt. In Season 2, he became a 2D character with more proportional features and gained a black eyebrow. After escaping the Void, Rob underwent dramatic changes. His head became grey, misshapen, and dodecahedral, with TV static covering portions. His eye turned pale yellow with a black lemniscate-shaped pupil. His body displayed static effects, with asymmetrical shoes -- one chartreuse wireframe, one dark grey prism. His clothing became polygonal with a rhombus-shaped hole in his shirt. His design routinely glitches, with static intensity changing based on emotions. From Season 5 onward, his pupil became ovular. After "The Future," he gained a pig tail (later removed). In "The Rewrite," he wore purple armor with spikes and eventually adopted a pure black appearance in 2D form.

## Personality
Originally sociable and friendly, Rob demonstrated some entitlement and irritability. After escaping the Void with amnesia, he became depressed and insecure. Upon regaining his memory, Rob became vengeful and bitter, viewing himself as Gumball's arch-nemesis. He sought revenge through increasingly destructive plans, driven by existential questions about identity and purpose. However, Rob showed complexity: he sacrificed reconciliation to undo damage in "The Rerun" and later attempted warning others about the Void threat despite poor communication skills hampering his efforts.
