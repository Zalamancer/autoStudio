# Vladus Lokowitchki

## Appearance
As a vengeful spirit (the Snatcher), he has a humanoid shape wearing a long green hooded cloak with prominent rips. His limbs are long and thin, and his face is gaunt -- almost like a decomposing corpse.

As a mortal, Vladus appears as an older man with white skin matching Carrie's body. He wears a train conductor's cap, a trench coat with a red undershirt, black pants, and brown boots.

In ghost form, he mostly retains his mortal appearance, the only differences being that his legs are replaced by ghostly tails and his hair constantly stands on end.

## Personality
He demonstrates deep affection for his daughter, embracing her warmly upon reunion. He was extremely aggressive and sadistic, though Carrie attributes this behavior to being trapped in the mirror for so long.
