# Patrick Fitzgerald

## Appearance
Patrick bears a strong resemblance to his daughter Penny, but is much larger and bulkier with more masculine features than his daughter, with large moose antlers. He wears brown sandals with socks.

Without his shell, his true form is a conglomerate of different things mashed together, resembling some sort of odd chimera. His inner form glows a shade of pink.

Early storyboard designs showed differences: a thinner shell, a mustache, and shorter legs.

## Personality
Patrick demonstrates strong protective instincts toward his family, particularly Penny, maintaining a very serious demeanor. His overprotectiveness stems from the family's shape-shifting nature -- he feared society would view them as monsters without their shells. His concerns proved valid when Penny experienced an unstable transformation into increasingly monstrous forms.

Initially viewing Gumball as a troublemaker, Patrick changes his perspective after Gumball saves Penny, though he regularly tries to find a reason to want to keep Gumball away from his daughter for unclear motives.

He runs his own construction company called Fitzgerald Co. Limited and maintains excellent physical fitness, evident through jogging appearances and his ability to break Gumball's ukulele in one episode.
