# Banana Barbara

## Appearance
Banana Barbara is an anthropomorphic banana. She is nearly identical to the rest of her family, with the exception of a blue and white polka-dot bow (which she wears to keep her head from falling apart due to her burnout), lipstick, and eyelashes. In season 2, she gained eyebrows and a lighter-colored peel.

## Personality
According to "The Mothers," Banana Barbara demonstrates limited intellect and poor awareness of her surroundings, with a notably creepy demeanor. "The Future" reveals she was once competent and intelligent before experiencing burnout from her internship at Chanax Inc., which caused her current behavioral changes. She also tends to interpret language literally at times.
