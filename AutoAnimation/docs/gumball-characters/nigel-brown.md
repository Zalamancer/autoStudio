# Nigel Brown

## Appearance
Principal Brown is a slug with a thick coat of brown fur that covers his entire body, except for his eyes. He wears thick round glasses. His early design featured purple fur instead. Beneath the fur lies tan-tinted skin and a humanoid body structure. Notable physical traits include a hidden nose and distinctive walking sounds caused by red high-heels he wears. He was revealed to have been born with a small butt and wore a wig to cover it after childhood bullying.

## Personality
Despite holding the principal position, Principal Brown displays a massive crush on Miss Simian, frequently neglecting school duties to pursue her. However, he occasionally demonstrates integrity by refusing to let her frame students falsely. He takes considerable pride in his appearance and sometimes intervenes in students' personal matters. He exhibits both antagonism and occasional support toward Gumball and Darwin, punishing them frequently yet occasionally defending them when circumstances warrant.
