# Sussie

## Appearance
Sussie is a chinkin with googly eyes which she can take off and put on at any time. She wears a white and periwinkle plaid dress with white shoes, and she is completely bald. Her eyes can appear at varying distances in some episodes. In season 2, she became taller.

## Personality
Sussie comes across as weird and obnoxious to peers, with sloppy eating habits and a tendency to talk excessively while making unusual noises. Despite this, she maintains friendships with cheerleaders. She is notably profound -- she provided meaningful insights about life's meaning when others couldn't. She is oblivious to bullying and indifferent to judgment, preferring to live authentically. She desires to live her life to the fullest and not worry about how people perceive her. She demonstrates creativity and kindness, giving gifts to classmates to help them see her perspective.
