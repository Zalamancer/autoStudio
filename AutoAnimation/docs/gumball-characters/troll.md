# Troll

## Appearance
The troll is a lilac, ogre-like creature. He dons a golden cape and shelters his wrists in golden bands. His eyes are an opaque yellow and his mouth contains only two teeth; his nose is quite gargantuan relative to the rest of Elmore's denizens.

## Personality
Since the troll is an allegory for an internet troll, his personality consists of characteristics typical of an inflammatory web dweller. The troll takes pleasure in angering people and spends his days spewing lies for the sake of getting a reaction, and then eating the people they anger. However, as is typical of most web flamers, the troll is very hypocritical, not being able to withstand irritation himself, as evidenced by his interaction with Gumball. Not only did the troll become aggravated by Gumball's obnoxious questions, but he also lost his power from listening to them.
