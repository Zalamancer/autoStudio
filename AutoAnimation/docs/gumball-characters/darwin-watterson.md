# Darwin Watterson

## Appearance
Darwin is an orange goldfish with arms and legs whose goldfish-shaped head takes up his entire body, with his fins and legs hanging down from it. He retains his goldfish tail and uses his fins as arms and hands, with long legs ending in plain feet typically covered by green and white sneakers.

The character possesses thick blubber coating providing pain resistance. His mouth interior is red with a red-orange tongue. Season 1 featured a shadow effect often mistaken for clothing. Season 2 brought a redesign with more transparent shadowing, thicker legs, and double eyelashes per eye instead of three. From Season 3 onward, his eyes became permanently round like the rest of his family. In the early prototype, Darwin was originally a 3D animated character.

## Personality
Darwin exhibits consistent positivity and optimism, always trying to see the best in most of the scenarios he is in. He demonstrates selflessness through acts designed to make others happy. He is described as grateful, content with his current life, and possessing idealistic views about people's inherent goodness.

The character maintains a strong sense of honesty and desires truthfulness despite potential consequences. His politeness sometimes overrides this honesty. Darwin serves as a moral compass, typically preventing siblings from wrongdoing and avoiding shady approaches.

He demonstrates significant sensitivity and empathy, showing deep affection toward family, particularly Gumball. This emotional attachment creates possessiveness and loneliness without loved ones. Despite his general happiness, Darwin can become angry when pushed beyond his limits, occasionally resorting to violence.
