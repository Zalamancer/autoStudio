# Larry Needlemeyer

## Appearance
Larry typically wears a green shirt, an orange tie, a red badge with a diagonal stripe down the middle, and an orange hat with red and white stripes. He has black pants and black shoes along with pink cheeks with freckles. His physique is notably slim, and his head appears disproportionately large and blocky. Originally designed as a rock character, series creator Ben Bocquelet clarified that Larry was supposed to be a rock but became an origami type of character instead. In season 1, rocky textures were visible, but these diminished starting in season 2, giving him a fully origami appearance. His animation style shifted to resemble stop-motion rather than fluid movement.

## Personality
Larry demonstrates strong work ethic and dedication, maintaining numerous jobs throughout Elmore while performing admirably in each role. He is characterized as serious and difficult to deceive, though he possesses a forgiving nature as demonstrated in certain episodes. Despite his current industrious reputation, Larry has a history of laziness. He frequently experiences sleep deprivation from working multiple shifts simultaneously. While generally providing service with a smile, he can become nervous and jumpy in certain situations. Additionally, he shows willingness to express displeasure when mistreated or disrespected by others.
