## Round 4 Categories That Completely Failed (0% approval)

### Minimal Clip-Path Reveals — 0/8
Diamond, Hex, Star, Cross, Curtain, Polygon, CircleGrow, Zigzag — all rejected.
**Why:** Geometric shapes growing from center are visually boring. The existing liked minimals (Fade, Mask, RevealLine, Rotate) have distinctive MOTION mechanics, not just shapes.

### Digital UI Disruption — 0/8
BlueScreen, PopupStorm, CaptchaResolve, PasswordReveal, ProgressCrash, NotificationFlood, AutoCorrect, ClipboardPaste — all rejected.
**Why:** The UI simulation overshadows the text. Text becomes secondary to the BSOD/popup/CAPTCHA environment. Compare with liked templates where text IS the effect (VHS Pause distorts THE TEXT, not a simulated VHS player).

### Stationery & Office — 0/8
StickyTape, PaperClip, RubberStamp, IndexCard, BinderTab, WhiteboardWrite, CorrectionTape, MemoPad — all rejected.
**Why:** Physical objects as containers for text don't work. The user sees "a paper clip with text" not "text doing something interesting." Same pattern as round 3's handcraft failures.

### Art Supply Effects — 0/8
SprayPaint, MarkerStroke, PaintRoller, PaletteKnife, BallpointPen, BrushCalligraphy, CharcoalRub, PastelSmear — all rejected.
**Why:** Drawing/painting simulations where the tool matters more than the text. The liked handcraft templates (Chalk, Pencil, Crayon) succeed because they reference universally known school experiences AND the text reveal is the focus.

### Internet Era Nostalgia — 0/8
MySpaceGlitter, TumblrFade, GeoCities, WindowsXP, DialUpConnect, AOLInstant, Web1Point0, WordArt — all rejected.
**Why:** Too much background/environment simulation. The aesthetic overwhelms the text. The user sees "a GeoCities page" not "text doing something."
