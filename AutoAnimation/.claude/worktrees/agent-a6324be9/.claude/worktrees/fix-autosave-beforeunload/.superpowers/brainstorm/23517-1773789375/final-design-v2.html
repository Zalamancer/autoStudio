Design Summary

  • Left panel — Presets only. No tabs. Toggle → Search → Quick Moves → Genre Feel → Cinema Shots
  • Right panel — All camera controls. Transform at frame → Camera & Lens → Optical sliders + toggles → Effects
  • Footer button — "Camera Settings →" opens right panel
  • Search — Unified across all three preset groups
  • Colors — All #4a7eff accent, #2a2a2a/#222 surfaces, #3a3a3a hover
  • First glance — User sees toggle + search + presets. Nothing else until they click "Camera Settings"
