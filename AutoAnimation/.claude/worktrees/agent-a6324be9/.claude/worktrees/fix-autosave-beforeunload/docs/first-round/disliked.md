# Disliked Templates (685 / 799)

685 templates rejected in first-round review (85.7% rejection rate).

## Breakdown
- **235 Kinetic** typography rejected
- **449 Scene** layout/card templates rejected (near-total rejection of scene category)
- **1 unrated** (missed)

## Key Rejection Patterns

### Almost ALL Scene/Card Templates Rejected (449/471 = 95%)
Only 22 scene templates survived. The overwhelming majority of "data card" and "info display" templates were rejected:
- ALL fitness/health cards (workout, step counter, exercise, etc.)
- ALL food/recipe cards (cooking, meal plan, calorie, etc.)
- ALL real estate cards (property, listing, open house, etc.)
- ALL e-commerce cards (product, cart, shipping, etc.)
- ALL travel cards (flight, hotel, destination, etc.)
- ALL crypto/finance cards (budget, savings, investment, etc.)
- ALL music player/album cards
- ALL weather/climate displays
- ALL DIY/craft cards
- ALL auto/car spec cards (except speedometer which is kinetic)
- ALL marketing/business analytics
- ALL architecture/interior design cards
- ALL eco/sustainability cards
- ALL photography cards (except camera viewfinder kinetic)
- ALL astrology/zodiac cards
- ALL pet/animal cards
- ALL beauty/fashion cards
- ALL event/celebration cards
- ALL gaming cards (except game-over)

### Disliked Kinetic Themes (235)
- ALL cultural/world art templates (Aboriginal, African, Arabic, Celtic, Chinese, etc.)
- ALL nature/weather backgrounds (aurora, autumn, cherry blossom, blizzard, etc.)
- ALL music-themed (bass drop, amplifier, equalizer, DJ deck, etc.)
- ALL food-themed kinetic (coffee stain, menu chalk, sizzle pop, etc.)
- ALL horror-specific (blood drip, blood drool, spider crawl, zombie, etc.)
- ALL meditation/spiritual (chakra, mandala, zen, lotus, etc.)
- ALL "basic" effects (bounce, carousel, color shift, bold impact, etc.)
- ALL 3D/perspective effects (cube rotate, parallax layers, etc.)
- ALL kid/cartoon themed (balloon, bubble pop, crayon draw, etc. — except crayon-write, jelly-wobble, star-burst)
